How I Work

Editing Approach

A simple process I follow on every project, whether it's a 30-second Reel or a 40-minute interview.

Step 01

Review & Organize Footage

Watch through the raw footage, note the best moments, and organize files before editing starts.

Step 02

Structure the Story

Build a rough cut that establishes pacing and flow, whether that's a short Reel or a longer video.

Step 03

Refine the Edit

Tighten transitions, sync audio, and fine-tune pacing so nothing feels slow or out of place.

Step 04

Sound & Visual Polish

Balance audio levels, add captions or graphics where needed, and apply consistent color.

Step 05

Export & Deliver

Export in the right format and specs for the destination platform, then share for feedback.
